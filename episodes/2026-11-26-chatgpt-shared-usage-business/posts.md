## Website article (augaster.com/digest)

### ChatGPT Business Offers 2x Usage as OpenAI Merges Chat and Work Limits

**What happened.** OpenAI is starting to merge ChatGPT's Chat and Work into one shared usage limit. At the same time, new ChatGPT Business customers who sign up by Nov 30 get 2x included usage and fast mode at no extra cost for all seats through Dec 31.

**How it works.** Under the shared limit, Chat and Work draw from one allowance instead of two. OpenAI plans to bring shared usage limits more broadly in December, according to its support page. Conversations with the Instant model stay unlimited and do not count toward the shared allowance.

**Availability and price.** The offer applies to new ChatGPT Business customers who sign up by Nov 30. It includes 2x included usage and fast mode at no extra cost for all seats through Dec 31. The Nov 30 deadline is stated on OpenAI's pricing page. We could not read OpenAI's help-center article on the merger directly, so the merger details come from press coverage.

**What it means for small businesses.** Most users are not expected to wait for usage resets more often. The catch is that a small fraction of the heaviest users may hit limits sooner. If your team includes a few people who lean on ChatGPT all day, they are the ones most likely to notice the change.

**Our take.** If you were already thinking about ChatGPT Business, the sign-up deadline is a practical reason to decide before Nov 30. Once you're in, keep an eye on usage among your heaviest users, and check what the shared limits look like when the wider rollout arrives in December.
