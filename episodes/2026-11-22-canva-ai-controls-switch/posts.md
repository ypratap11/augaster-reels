Canva has added AI Controls, and the headline feature is a simple one: a switch to turn generative AI off.

What's new:
• A setting that turns generative AI features on or off. It's at Profile > Settings > Permissions, labelled "Enable generative AI features".
• A setting that reduces how much AI-generated content appears in the content library. It's at Profile > Your Account > AI Personalization.
• Background Remover and Magic Grab stay available with generative AI off. They work with the content of an existing image and don't generate anything new.

The catch: the library setting reduces the chance of AI-generated content appearing in search. It does not guarantee AI content is excluded.

## Website article (augaster.com/digest)

### Canva Adds a Switch to Turn Generative AI Off

**What happened.** Canva has added two new AI settings. The first turns generative AI features off or on. The second reduces how much AI-generated content appears in the content library. Engadget and PetaPixel both covered the announcement, which was dated Nov. 19, 2026. We could not fetch Canva's own page to confirm the date, so it rests on those two reports.

**How it works.** The generative AI switch is at Profile > Settings > Permissions, labelled "Enable generative AI features". The library setting is at Profile > Your Account > AI Personalization. Background Remover and Magic Grab stay available when generative AI is switched off. They work with the content of an existing image and don't generate anything new. The library setting reduces the chance of AI-generated content appearing in search, but it does not guarantee AI content is excluded.

**What it means for small businesses.** If your brand, your clients or your industry call for human-made work, you can now set that preference inside Canva. You also keep the editing tools that don't create anything new. The catch is the library setting. It lowers the odds of seeing AI content in search but doesn't guarantee you won't, so you still need to check what you pick and publish.

**Our take.** Decide your AI policy first, then set the switch to match. If you have a team or freelancers using a shared Canva account, tell them what you chose and why. Then spot-check library assets, since that filter is not a guarantee.
