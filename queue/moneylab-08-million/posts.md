## Website article (augaster.com/digest)

### Can $10,000 become $1 million?

**The question.** Can $10,000 grow into $1,000,000? We assumed a steady 7% a year, compounded monthly, with no taxes or fees.

**Alone.** To reach $1 million, $10,000 has to grow 100 times. At 7% a year that takes about 68.1 years.

**With contributions.** Adding $500 a month gets the balance to $1 million in about 35.6 years. At that point the original $10,000 on its own would be worth about $111,064.

**What it means for you.** Early on, contributions drive growth; later, compounding does. Starting earlier and contributing steadily changes the timeline more than reaching for a slightly higher return.

**Our take.** Real returns vary year to year, so treat this as an illustration of the math. This is education, not financial advice.
