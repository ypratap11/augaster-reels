# @augaster.ai growth playbook (Nov 2026)

The five-prompt "faceless channel" playbook (competitor analysis, viral ideas, transcript
study, cover/title analysis, 30-day calendar), applied to Augaster AI Digest.
Goal: build the "established presence" Instagram requires for monetization (see
help.instagram.com/512371932629820) with real followers, no bought engagement.

## 1. Competitor analysis
Accounts in our space fall into three groups:

| Group | Typical content | What works | Gap we can own |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI-tools creators (prompt lists, "faceless channel" posts) | Carousels of prompts, "I made $X with AI" hooks | Carousels get saved and shared; big-result hooks | Unverified income claims; nothing for a real shop, salon or contractor |
| AI news pages | Headline reels, daily news | Speed, volume | No "what it means for my business"; no sources |
| Small-business marketing pages | Generic tips, Canva graphics | Practical, relatable | Little AI; rarely current |

**Our position:** verified AI news + practical AI how-to, both for small-business owners,
with sources. Nobody combines fact-checked news with copy-paste tools.

Data points (vendor studies, treat loosely): Reels win on reach; carousels win on saves and
engagement rate. So reels bring new people and carousels make them follow.

## 2. Idea bank (formats with hooks)
Reels (news, already automated, 7 rotating beats in `pick_story.py`), plus carousels:
1. 5 AI prompts every small business owner should keep (done: `carousels/2026-11-20-5-prompts-small-business`)
2. Reply to Google reviews with AI: 3 prompts by tone
3. 7 free AI tools for a one-person business (only tools with a free tier we've verified)
4. AI scam warning signs: voice-clone calls, fake invoices, deepfake bosses
5. ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini for small-business tasks (task by task, no rankings we can't back)
6. Prompts for restaurants / salons / contractors / realtors (one carousel per industry)
7. "This week in AI" recap of our 3 best reels as slides
8. How to write a job post, policy or SOP with AI
9. 5 AI mistakes that cost small businesses money
10. Turn one blog post into a week of content with AI

## 3. What makes our reels hold attention (hook study)
- First 2 seconds: say the consequence, not the company ("Your Google reviews just got a robot reply button").
- One idea per reel; the "what it means for you" segment is the reason to watch.
- End on a reason to follow, not just a link.

## 4. Covers and titles
- Cover = one bold line under 7 words + the gold highlighter on the key words.
- Numbers and "you" outperform brand names in titles.
- Keep the Augaster star in the corner of every cover so the grid looks like one series.
