**What happened.** GoDaddy announced the release of Airo AI Builder on Nov. 16, 2026. GoDaddy describes it as an agentic operating system for small businesses. Users can build websites, web apps and online stores without coding, then keep refining them through conversation. GoDaddy marketing VP Selina Bieber said it lets people create, test ideas and make updates through natural conversation.

**How it works.** You can create using written or voice prompts. Airo AI Builder connects to more than 90 third-party tools, including Slack, Notion and HubSpot. For online stores, you can accept payments through GoDaddy Payments or connect Stripe, and more payment providers are coming. GoDaddy Payments is available only in the US and Canada, and proceeds can reach a bank account as soon as the same business day. You can also download or export your code at any time.

There was a beta phase in summer 2026. GoDaddy analyzed an anonymized sample of more than 100,000 customer-created projects from a six-week period of that beta. Health, Wellness and Beauty made up 13% of creations, Home & Property 12% and Business Services 11%. Together with Health, those categories accounted for more than one-third of creations. Roughly one-tenth of beta creations were Rebuild/Reimagine projects, meaning users who already had a website and wanted to improve it.

**Availability and price.** Airo AI Builder has a free tier. Paid plans start at $9.99 per month, and higher tiers with more credits and site allowances are available. It's available in more than 150 countries with multi-language support. The full integrated online store experience (product catalogue, shopping cart, checkout, shipping and sales tax tools) is currently supported only in the U.S. and Canada. Elsewhere, customers can connect Stripe for service payments or deposits.

On the business side, GoDaddy said Airo surpassed $100 million in annualized bookings run rate in the third quarter, more than doubling from the previous quarter. That figure covers Airo overall. It is a bookings run rate, not revenue, and not AI Builder alone.

**What it means for small businesses.** If you don't have a developer or a big budget, this lowers the barrier to getting a website or simple store online. You can describe what you want, adjust it by chatting, and connect tools you may already use. The ability to export your code is a useful safeguard against being locked in. The catch is location. If you're outside the US and Canada, you won't get the full integrated store experience, though you can connect Stripe for service payments or deposits. The beta mix of projects (health and beauty, home services, business services) suggests the tool is already being used by local service businesses.

**Our take.** Start on the free tier and build something real, such as a service page or a small shop, before you pay for anything. Check that the payment options and store features work in your country. Treat the beta statistics and run-rate figures as GoDaddy's own numbers rather than proof the tool will fit your business.
