**What happened.** Sierra released Personal Agent Protocol (Poppy) version 1.0 on November 13, 2026. Shopify has enabled Poppy for every merchant on its platform, and every Shopify store automatically publishes a Poppy file. Sierra notes that Shopify powers businesses in more than 175 countries. Poppy isn't new: it was first announced on October 6, 2026, with Meta, Genesys, Instinct, NiCE, Rocket, Shopify, Stripe and Walmart. A draft followed on October 9, 2026, along with 35 additional design partners. Version 1.0 adds 60 new design partners, bringing the total to more than 100. The new group includes Cisco, Delta Air Lines, Intuit, Twilio and Zoom.

**How it works.** Poppy defines how a personal agent discovers a company and interacts with it through its APIs, websites or company agents. Customers sign in through OAuth and control what their personal agents can do. Meta's Muse agent looks for Poppy on every site it visits, and it looks for Poppy first, before other methods. Poppy is on for everyone who uses Muse. It complements Shopify's Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP). UCP handles shopping: products, cart and checkout. Poppy handles the agent arriving, identifying itself and learning what it is allowed to do. Under the Poppy commerce extension, which Shopify co-authored, the agent must ask the customer before taking certain actions.

**Availability and price.** Shopify merchants need no extra implementation or setup for Poppy. The sources we reviewed do not state any price.

**What it means for small businesses.** If you run a Shopify store, your store is already publishing a Poppy file, so agents that look for it, such as Meta's Muse, can find it without any work from you. Early live examples show where this is heading. Mercury agents can check balances and start payments, which still go through the account's approval rules. Turkish Airlines agents can check fares and find business-class upgrades, which customers approve before paying. The catch is that agents must ask customers before certain actions, so some steps will still wait on a human decision.

**Our take.** Zero setup is good news, but don't treat it as a strategy. We'd suggest checking how your store appears to agents, reviewing your policies, and watching how customer approvals play out. If you're not on Shopify, keep an eye on whether your platform adopts Poppy.
