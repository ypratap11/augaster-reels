**What happened.** On Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026, Google announced computer use in the Gemini app for Mac. The feature lets Gemini complete multi-step tasks across local files, desktop apps and the web. The announcement came in a Google blog post by Michael Friedman, Group Product Manager, Gemini App.

**How it works.** You describe what you need, and Gemini handles the clicking, typing, menus and forms. It works in an isolated background window with a live preview in the chat, so your main screen and cursor stay free for your own work. You choose which apps Gemini can access, can watch every step live, can pause at any time, and approve key actions before they happen. Google's named use cases include batch colour-grading photos or setting up 3D scenes, filling out shipping quote forms from local PDFs, building a Google Slides presentation from live dashboard data, and batch-listing items for sale on resale platforms.

**Availability and price.** The feature is rolling out in English in the U.S. It requires a Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra subscription and an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS Sequoia 15.0 or later. It is available for personal Google accounts, with users 18 or older.

**What it means for small businesses.** Many small businesses lose hours to repetitive desktop work like filling in quote forms, listing products or assembling reports from dashboards. A tool that can operate your apps while you keep working could take on some of that. The catch is that it's limited to certain subscriptions, Macs, a U.S. English rollout and personal Google accounts, so not every team can use it yet.

**Our take.** Start small. Pick one repetitive, low-risk task, watch Gemini work through it step by step, and keep approving key actions yourself. If it saves you time and gets the details right, widen the scope gradually.
