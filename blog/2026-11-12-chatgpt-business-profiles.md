**What happened.** OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT as a beta on Tuesday, November 10, 2026. Businesses can claim their profile through a new dashboard called Business Hub and update the information customers see. OpenAI says ChatGPT reaches 1.2 billion people a week. That is a figure for ChatGPT overall, not for Business Hub.

**How it works.** Eligible U.S. ecommerce and online service businesses can publish a Business Agent at launch, and the U.S. restriction is described as "for now." The agent answers customer questions about the business's own products and policies. It is built from instructions and context the business supplies, such as a product feed and tone guidelines. Digiday adds that OpenAI does not allow businesses to bring their own agents. The agent conversation is labelled separately in ChatGPT, so shoppers can tell they have moved from the general assistant to one speaking for the business.

Businesses can also sponsor a Business Agent and make it the destination for a ChatGPT ad. A shopper who clicks the ad can start a chat with the business's agent.

The Business Hub dashboard shows mentions, profile views, website clicks and discovery topics, which are common themes in conversations where ChatGPT cites the business's site. How a mention is counted depends on the business type. For an online business, ChatGPT must reference its website. For a local business, showing the location in a result counts. Businesses do not see users' conversations or Business Agent chats, only aggregate themes and counts.

**Availability and price.** Business Hub and agents are free at launch. OpenAI's vice president of monetization, Benji Shomair, did not rule out charging in future. The profile-claim beta is available wherever ChatGPT is available, though VentureBeat qualifies this to markets where OpenAI supports the required verification. Local stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing U.S. listing.

**What it means for small businesses.** Business profiles are positioned as similar to, and competing with, Google Business Profile. For a small business, that means a new place where customers may find you and ask questions. The catch is that claiming a profile gives no guarantee of better placement or more sales. OpenAI says ChatGPT keeps making independent recommendations. The free price could also change, and the agent only works from the information you give it.

**Our take.** If you're eligible, claim your profile and fill it in carefully. Keep your product information and policies accurate, because the agent depends on them. Treat the first few weeks as a test, and check the dashboard numbers before you decide how much effort to put in.
