**What happened.** Apple is now selling Intelligence Usage Packs for $49.99. The packs let people buy more AI usage in Apple's Creator Studio apps. The launch was reported on November 9, 2026. Until now, all Apple Intelligence features were free on Apple devices, so this is Apple's first take on charging for AI features.

**How it works.** The packs apply only to Apple Creator Studio apps for now. Yahoo Finance describes them as being for active Creator Studio subscribers, while 9to5Mac frames the details a little differently, so the exact eligibility rules are not fully confirmed. 9to5Mac also reports that Apple has said Apple Intelligence will not stay free forever.

**What it means for small businesses.** If you or your team use Creator Studio apps, AI usage may soon be something you pay for and need to plan around. The catch is that this looks like a first step. Costs could creep up if Apple charges for more AI features later. That is our reading of the situation, not something Apple has announced.

**Our take.** Track how much AI you actually use in these apps before you spend anything. Buy a pack only if it clearly saves you time or money, and keep an eye on what Apple says about future pricing.
