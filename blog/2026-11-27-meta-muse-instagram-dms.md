**What happened.** Meta integrated its Muse AI agent into Instagram DMs, so users can chat with Muse in a DM thread. Walmart also partnered with Meta, so customers can shop Walmart's Black Friday deals through Muse.

**How it works.** The key detail is where the shopping happens: inside an AI agent rather than a search page. Muse accounted for 51% of all agentic shopping orders, ahead of Grok at 24% and ChatGPT at 22%. The Decoder attributes that data to Forter Intelligence, and it is a share of AI-agent orders, not of all online orders.

The picture is less simple at Amazon. Amazon blocked Meta's Muse shopping agent in September 2026. Amazon and Meta are reportedly in talks to lift the block, and no deal has been announced. Ad revenue and control over search results are the main sticking points, and Amazon's advertising business brings in $68.6 billion a year. Amazon's decision to block third-party AI agents from making purchases contrasts with Walmart's approach of welcoming them.

**What it means for small businesses.** Customers may increasingly shop where they already chat, which could make Instagram DMs a bigger part of how people find and buy products. The catch is that access depends on deals between giant platforms, and those are still changing. The 51% figure also covers only AI-agent orders, so it is not a measure of online shopping overall.

**Our take.** Don't rebuild your business around Muse yet. Keep your product details clear and consistent so an AI agent can describe them accurately, and watch how the Amazon talks end. That will show how open this kind of shopping really becomes.
