**What happened.** On November 17, 2026, Google announced an expansion of Tasks in Gemini. The announcement came in a blog post written by Josh Woodward, Google's VP of Labs, Gemini app and AI Studio. The feature is older than the expansion: it was announced at Google I/O 2026 in May as Gemini Spark, a 24/7 personal agent. TechCrunch says it first went to select testers and later to U.S. Google AI Ultra subscribers. Gemini Spark was then folded into the main Gemini app as Task mode, a redesign 9to5Google reported on October 12, 2026, with Chat and Task buttons above the prompt box.

**How it works.** Tasks in Gemini is a toggle that switches the Gemini app from Chat to Task. In Task mode, Gemini acts on the user's behalf instead of only answering questions. It can call businesses and book appointments, and it can complete purchases using a Google Pay integration. It can also sign into websites or portals for the user. Google ties this to a Google Password Manager integration, and TechCrunch says it happens securely if prompted. Users can also text Gemini to give it tasks, on Android or iOS, via Settings > Channels > Text with Gemini, with a verified phone number.

**Availability and price.** Tasks is now free of charge to hundreds of millions of people, using the new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model. The RCS text integration needs a US phone number with an eligible mobile carrier.

**What it means for small businesses.** More of your customers may have an AI assistant that can call you or book appointments with you. That makes it worth checking that your hours, services and booking details are clear and easy to find, whether a person or an AI is asking. The catch is that texting Gemini requires a US phone number with an eligible carrier, so not everyone will be able to use every part of it.

**Our take.** Test Task mode on your own errands first, such as booking a service or making a call you would normally make yourself. That will show you what customers may experience. Then tidy up your own booking information so it works well for both people and AI assistants.
